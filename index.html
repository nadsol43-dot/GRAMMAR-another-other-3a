Rule card — открыть / закрыть
another = ещё один, ещё одна (любая) → + существительное в единственном числе: another pen, another cup.
other = другие → перед существительным во мн. ч. или неисчисляемым: other people, other side…
the other = второй, другой (их всего два / конкретный второй): the other hand, the other book.
others = другие (без существительного, в общем смысле): Some like tea, others like coffee.
the others = остальные (конкретная известная группа): Two went home, the others stayed.
each other / one another = друг друга: They help each other.
Тест: ① есть the → «второй / остальные из известной группы»; ② есть существительное после → other / the other; ③ существительного нет → others / the others / another (one).

Model answers — все шесть форм по одному разу

Model 1 (проще)

  1. I have two favourite films. One is «The Irony of Fate» and the other is «Interstellar».
  2. I have five close friends. Two of them are good at sport, and the others play music.
  3. When I want another cup of tea, I usually make it myself.
  4. I often talk to my family about other things — school, films and plans for the weekend.
  5. Some of my classmates want to go to university, and the others want to start working.
  6. My best friend and I always help each other with English.

Model 2 (богаче, B1)

  1. One is a comedy I’ve seen ten times, and the other is a documentary about the ocean.
  2. Two of them are good at sport, and the others prefer music.
  3. When I want another cup of tea, I take my favourite big mug and go back to my homework.
  4. I often talk to my family about other people’s news — who moved to another city, who got married.
  5. Some of my classmates want to go to university, and the others are still choosing.
  6. We always help each other with English: I explain grammar and she checks my pronunciation.

Важно: в п. 4 возможен и вариант I often talk to my family about others — «про других людей» (реже, но грамматично). Тогда в п. 4 стоит others, и набор шести форм всё равно полный.

Step 4 · Ты — рассказчик not scored

Расскажи свою историю (6–8 предложений), используя минимум пять разных форм, и хотя бы одну «друг друга».

Model story — шесть форм: another · the other · the others · each other · other · others

Last autumn my family and I moved to another flat in a quieter area. Our new flat has only two rooms, so my brother and I share one and our parents have the other. At first I didn’t like the place: some of the neighbours were very friendly, and the others hardly said hello. Now I know them all. My brother and I decorated our room ourselves and never argued with each other. On sunny days we play football outside; on other days we stay at home and watch films. My mother wants to buy another sofa for the living room, because the old one is too small for all four of us. Some of my classmates still live near our old flat, and others have moved away, so I don’t see them very often. But my brother and I always do our homework together and help each other, and that makes any new place feel like home.

Как считать формы: another — moved to another flat · the other — parents have the other (of two rooms) · the others — some of the neighbours … the others · each other — argued with each other · other — on other days · others — some classmates … others. В конце снова each other — повтор не считается новой формой.

Watch out · самые частые ошибки

another people ✗ → other people ✓ (another + только единственное число)
others people ✗ → other people ✓ (others — без существительного)
an other ✗ → another ✓
the other при трёх и более предметах → the others / another
each another ✗ → each other / one another ✓
others ≠ the others: some people → others (список открыт); some of my friends / five students in our group → the others (весь остаток). Тест: подставьте the rest — звучит как правда → the others.
